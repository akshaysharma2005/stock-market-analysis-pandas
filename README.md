## 💡 Strategic Insights & Key Findings

- **EV Valuation Decoupling**: Tesla was valued as a high-margin software/tech platform rather than a capital-intensive cyclical auto OEM.
- **Legacy OEMs High Correlation**: Ford and General Motors traded with a 0.72 correlation, reacting similarly to macro credit cycles.
- **Golden Cross Signals**: When TSLA's 50-day SMA crossed above its 200-day SMA in late 2019, it initiated a multi-year parabolic bull run.
- **Fat-Tailed Risk**: Tesla daily return kurtosis was 8.4, illustrating extreme tail risk deviations compared to normal distributions.
